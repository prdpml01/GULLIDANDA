Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Welcome to Gulli Danda ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game at https://prdpml01.github.io/GULLIDANDA/.

1. Information We Collect

When you register to play Gulli Danda, we collect:

  • Name: To display on the leaderboard and to identify you during gameplay.
  • Mobile Number: Used as your unique login ID.
  • Game Statistics: Your scores, wins, losses, and match history to show your progress.
  • Device Info: Browser type, device type, and general location (country-level) for analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save your game progress and show your stats
  • To match you with other online players
  • To display the leaderboard
  • To improve the game experience

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Realtime Database: For storing game data and account info. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Analytics: For understanding how players use our game. See Google Privacy.
  • Google AdSense: To display ads that keep the game free. See AdSense Privacy.

4. Cookies & Advertising

We use cookies to serve relevant ads through Google AdSense. Google may use your information to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase servers. We never sell your personal information to anyone.

6. Children's Privacy

Gulli Danda is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

You can delete your account anytime by emailing us at the address below. Your data will be removed within 7 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
