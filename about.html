About Gulli Danda

A free online Desi game — bachpan ka khel, ab phone par

Gulli Danda is a free online browser game that brings back one of India's most loved traditional street games. Remember playing with a wooden stick (danda) and a small piece (gulli) in the gully outside your home? We have recreated that exact feeling for your phone and computer — no install needed, no sign-up required, just tap and play.

🎮 What Is Gulli Danda?

Gulli Danda is a traditional Indian sport played with two wooden sticks. The longer stick (danda) is used to hit the smaller stick (gulli). Players score points based on how far they hit the gulli. It has been played in villages and small towns across India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Nepal for generations. It is a simple, exciting game of timing and power.

✨ Features of Our Game

  • Single Player Mode: Play level by level, unlock new stages, and beat your own high score.
  • Online Multiplayer: Challenge your friends or other players in real-time 1v1 matches.
  • Local Multiplayer: Play with 2, 3, or 4 friends on the same device.
  • Global Leaderboard: See how you rank against players from all over the world.
  • Customization: Unlock new player skins, danda designs, and speed boosts with in-game coins.
  • Voice Effects: Hindi voice reactions make every shot feel special — "Kamaal ka shot!", "Seedha dande pe!"
  • Offline Play: Once loaded, you can play Single Player even without internet.
  • Mobile-Friendly: Optimized for phones with fullscreen landscape support.

🎯 How to Play

  • Step 1: Tap the "TAP ANYWHERE" screen to enter the game in fullscreen.
  • Step 2: Choose Single Player, Local Multiplayer, or Online.
  • Step 3: Press the screen to toss the gulli up — release when the power meter hits the green zone.
  • Step 4: Press again to swing the danda at the right moment to hit the gulli as far as possible.
  • Step 5: Choose your shot type — PUSH (safe), DRIVE (mid), or LOFT (risky/high).
  • Step 6: Score as many points as you can. One "OUT" ends your innings.

💡 Tips to Score Higher

  • The green zone on the power meter gives maximum shot power — practice to stop it perfectly.
  • Time your swing when the gulli is closest to the hit zone circle for a "PERFECT!" shot.
  • LOFT shots go farther but are riskier — good for beating a high target.
  • Use speed boosts from the market to add up to +20% distance.
  • Buy extra lives so you get another chance after an OUT.
