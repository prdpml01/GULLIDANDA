About Gulli Danda Championship

Gulli Danda is India's most beloved traditional street game, now brought to life in a modern online experience.

🎯 Our Mission: Keep India's traditional games alive by giving them a fun, competitive online platform where anyone can play from anywhere in the world.

🎮 About the Game

Gulli Danda Championship is a modern mobile-friendly version of the classic gulli-danda game. Features include:

  • 5 difficulty levels from Rookie to Legend
  • Online multiplayer with live opponent tracking
  • Coin toss to decide who bats first
  • Global leaderboard
  • Local multiplayer for 2-5 players
  • Realistic animated crowds and stadium atmosphere

👨‍💻 About the Developer

My name is Pradeep Singh, and I'm passionate about bringing traditional Indian games into the digital world. Gulli Danda Championship is my tribute to the games we all played as kids on our streets and playgrounds.
