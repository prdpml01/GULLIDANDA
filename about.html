Privacy Policy — Gulli Danda

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Gulli Danda (gullidanda.co.in) is a free online game created by Pradeep Singh. This page explains what information the game uses and how it is handled. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: If you register, we store your username, a hashed (one-way encrypted) password, your scores, coins, lives, in-game purchases, and match statistics.
  • Guest play: If you play as a guest, your progress is stored only on your device's local storage, not on our servers.
  • Device storage: We use your browser's local storage to remember your login and game progress so you don't have to log in every time.
  • Online matches: During a live online match, your username, scores, and live gameplay data are shared with your opponent so the match can work.
  • Analytics: We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how many people play and how the game is used. This includes anonymous statistics like page views and device type.

2. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (database and analytics)
  • Google AdSense (advertising)

These services have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

4. In-Game Coins and Items

Coins and items in the game have no real-world money value. They cannot be exchanged for cash, refunded, or transferred between accounts.

5. Children's Privacy

This game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your Choices

You can stop using the game at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, email us at prdpml01@gmail.com with your username.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices including hashed passwords and secure HTTPS connections. However, no online service can guarantee 100% security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
